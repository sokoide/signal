Async-signal-safe examples (ok in handlers):
  _exit(), write(), read(), close(), fcntl(), kill(),
  sigaction(), signal(), sigprocmask(), alarm(), abort()

UNSAFE in signal handlers (can deadlock or corrupt state):
  printf(), malloc(), free(), exit(), fopen(), fclose(),
  pthread_mutex_lock(), pthread_mutex_unlock()
Conditionally safe: longjmp()/siglongjmp() only under POSIX's restrictions

Rule of thumb: do as little as possible in a handler.
Set a volatile sig_atomic_t flag and let main() do the rest.
