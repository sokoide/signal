Summary of POSIX signal inheritance rules:
  fork():  signal handlers are inherited.
  fork():  signal mask is inherited.
  fork():  pending signals are CLEARED for the child.
  fork():  alternate stack inheritance is implementation-defined
           (inherited on Linux, often cleared on macOS).
  exec():  caught handlers reset to SIG_DFL.
  exec():  ignored handlers (SIG_IGN) remain ignored.
  exec():  signal mask is preserved.
  exec():  alternate signal stack is cleared.
